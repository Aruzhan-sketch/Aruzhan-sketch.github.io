RESEARCH FOCUS

Long before a survivor reaches a shelter or a hotline, she has already rehearsed the conversation a hundred times: in her head, in the mirror, and increasingly, with a chatbot.

Understanding technology, agency, and support systems for vulnerable populations

I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy at Nazarbayev University, exploring the intersection of technology, human support systems, and behavioral change. My research examines how digital tools and AI-powered support mechanisms shape decision-making and resilience for vulnerable populations.

Through qualitative and participatory research methodologies, I investigate how survivors prepare for and approach seeking help, and the role emerging technologies play in these critical moments of vulnerability and agency.

About Me

I am a political scientist and researcher with a passion for understanding how technology intersects with human vulnerability, resilience, and support systems. My work centers on the experiences of survivors and marginalized populations, examining both the barriers they face and the innovative solutions that can help.

As a PhD candidate at Nazarbayev University, I employ participatory and qualitative research methodologies to ensure that the voices of those most affected by policy decisions are at the center of my work.
