RESEARCH FOCUS

What does it mean to live a good woman's life in Kazakhstan?

Understanding women's agency, expectations, and choices across Central Asia

I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy at Nazarbayev University, focused on understanding how expectations shape women's life choices and trajectories in Kazakhstan. My research examines the complex interplay between family, community, and state actors in influencing key life decisions.

Through in-depth qualitative research and community-based participatory approaches, I explore women's agency, resilience, and the social structures that define what it means to live a "good life" in contemporary Central Asia.
